# 0.6.0

- **Antigravity joins the office.** Connect Antigravity from Connect agents, and its agents and subagents walk in like the others. Its hooks only report: they never change what the agent does.
- **Teams across tools.** When an agent in T3 Code hands a task to another agent, even in another tool, that agent sits with it as a subagent and reports back when it is done.
- **More agents from T3 Code.** Codex chats that T3 Code starts now show up. Cursor agents get the name of their task and show when they finish.
- **Broken connections say so.** If a tool stops reporting, for example after you move the repo or update a plugin, the office tells you and Reconnect repairs it in one click.
- **Opens while macOS asks.** The office no longer waits behind a folder-access request, and the request now says why the office reads your project folders. Nothing leaves your Mac.
- **Follow finds the agent.** Follow takes the camera to the agent's floor or the roof, and follows it when it takes the lift.
- **A livelier city.** Clouds drift over the rooftops, roof lights blink, and now and then a helicopter crosses behind the office.
- **VR (beta).** Walk through the office in a Meta Quest. The Enter VR button says why VR did not start and what to do.
- **Your agents first in Customize.** Name and dress your agents at the top, and add your own 3D models from a folder the office opens for you.
- **Smaller fixes.** Agents known only from their logs sprint back to their desk too, the city you picked stays when another tab changes a setting, and the office tour points at the right things in an empty office.
